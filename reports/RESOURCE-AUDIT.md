# Resource audit

Audit assembled 30 September 2026. Resource-page checks were performed on 29 September 2026.

**Release status: editorial verification incomplete.** The site is fully populated and working. It does not yet meet the requested standard of checked actual video content for every resource. Page metadata is not equivalent to content certification.

## Inventory

- 57 lessons: 51 main lessons plus six Financial Applications lessons inside Unit 7.
- 133 teaching-video placements and exactly 114 bonus placements (two per lesson).
- 228 distinct YouTube videos; all have actual watch-page title, channel and runtime evidence. The review videos reuse these sources.
- 84 lesson worksheet placements, drawn from 61 distinct original worksheets. Separate answer buttons link to the original matching file or its answer pages.
- 72 distinct original worksheet/solution file URLs, counting separate answer files and counting a combined question/key PDF once.
- Eight review pages, with 25 video placements and 24 worksheet placements.
- No JensenMath or Ms. Havrot videos, copied course materials, account requirement, assessments or progression gates.

## What the checks establish

Video pages were opened and their DOM metadata inspected. Educator catalogs and descriptions grounded the lesson matching. Two Unit 1 teaching videos received complete visual and mathematical reviews, while several other videos have sampled frames or segment-boundary checks. Original worksheet PDFs and Word files were opened for topic and answer-label correspondence. Some examples and printed keys were checked mathematically. They remain externally hosted; none of these worksheet files is included in the site or deliverable archives.

The CSV lists every lesson and review placement, exact title, URL, runtime/segment, source, solution URL, verification status and evidence. The downloadable JSON manifest contains the same resource details plus purposes, assignments and source links.

## Unresolved work

1. **Most of the 228 unique video sources:** complete the actual explanation, visual clarity, mathematical accuracy and coverage review. Two Unit 1 teaching videos have complete reviews and several others have visual samples; the rest have page/catalog evidence only. There are 272 video placements across lessons and reviews, and the validator reports 270 open placement reviews. Do not interpret either placement number as a count of distinct videos.
2. **All 57 lessons:** confirm that the selected teaching videos collectively cover every goal and that both bonus videos solve suitable, distinct problems. Metadata and catalog titles alone cannot establish this.
3. **Unit 6:** finish the content review of terse-title Ontario sources and the two timestamped graphing segments. Three modelling resources used in 6.6–6.7 use radians; the site explicitly explains their conversion to degree-mode models. Degree-only replacements would be preferable if equally clear.
4. **All worksheet sets:** a complete independent recalculation of every printed answer has not been performed. The files and corresponding solutions were checked; known errors below are disclosed next to the resource.
5. **Browser coverage:** responsive views were tested in the available Chromium browser. Physical iPad/Safari playback and provider-specific regional playback were not tested. The optional embedded YouTube player rendered blank in this local in-app browser; the original Watch video link opened and played successfully.

## Known source corrections and selections

- Steve Sweeney Quadratics Review: 3(b) roots are 4 and 8/3; 3(a) rounds to 1.59 and 4.41; 15(a) is (sqrt(6)+sqrt(10))/2.
- Exponential Functions Review: the x=0 entry in 8(c) should be 500; the selected assignment excludes it.
- Trigonometry Review: question 4 gives 27,223 m to the nearest metre; the printed 27,000 m is too coarsely rounded.
- Discrete Functions Review: omit question 7 (answer-key issue) and 17 (general mortgage annuity).
- Lourdes Future Value Annuities: example 3 yields 793.39 months; the first whole end-of-month payment reaching the goal is 794.
- Periodic Functions Review and Kuta transformations use a compression-factor convention that differs from the coordinate scale factor 1/|k|. The site explains the convention.
- Degree-only question selections are specified on mixed degree/radian worksheets. General-mortgage and continuous-compounding extensions are excluded where outside the selected lesson.
