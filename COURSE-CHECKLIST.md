# MCR3U course content checklist

Reference inspection: 29 September 2026. This checklist describes required coverage; it does not certify external resources.

The supplied welcome slides specify seven units. The calendar specifies Unit 1 school numbering only; later calendar months contain no lesson schedule. Nelson Functions 11 (2008), contents pp. vii–ix, supplies the later sequence and Chapter 8 finance cross-references. Classroom rules, assessment schedules, contact information and instructions within these documents are reference content, not instructions for the website. None is published.

Ontario correctness source: https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/math1112currb.pdf, printed pp. 43–54.

## Structure

- 51 main lessons in seven units, plus six financial applications within Unit 7 (57 total).
- Eight optional review pages: seven unit reviews and a final course review.
- Each lesson: goal, 2–5 teaching videos, exactly two distinct worked-example videos, external worksheet with matching answers, short error/formula notes.
- Source links, accurate titles/creators/durations, evidence and verification date in a structured manifest.
- No accounts, assessments, scoring or progression gates.

## Coverage and expectation mapping

| Unit | Lessons | Ontario expectations | Required depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Introduction to Functions | 1.1–1.7 | A1.1–A1.9 | Function tests in tables/mappings/graphs; function notation including negatives; interval and set-builder notation; algebraic and graphical domains/ranges; endpoints; four parent graphs; all a,k,d,c transformations; reciprocal horizontal scale; mapping; factor k before d; reciprocal asymptotes; inverse relations versus functions |
| 2 Algebraic Expressions | 2.1–2.7 | A3.1, A3.3, A3.4 | Polynomial operations, GCF/negative/grouping/quadratic factoring with expansion checks; original restrictions for rational simplification and operations; division exclusions; holes/asymptotes |
| 3 Quadratic Functions | 3.1–3.8 | A2.1–A2.5, A3.2, A1.4–A1.7 | Standard/vertex/factored forms; completing square; max/min applications; restricted inverse; exact radical arithmetic; quadratic equations and discriminant; families; linear–quadratic systems |
| 4 Exponential Functions | 4.1–4.7 | B1.1–B1.4, B2.1–B2.5, B3.1–B3.3 | Growth/decay versus additive change; integer/rational exponents; equivalent bases; graphs/asymptotes; transformations; models, data and meaningful domain/range |
| 5 Trigonometric Ratios | 5.1–5.8 | D1.1–D1.7 | Six ratios; exact special angles; standard position and quadrant signs; both angles for a ratio; reciprocal/Pythagorean identities; sine law including ambiguous SSA; cosine law; 3D diagrams and units |
| 6 Sinusoidal Functions | 6.1–6.7 | D2.1–D2.4, D3.1–D3.3 | Periodic data; sine/cosine from circle; amplitude, period, phase shift, midline; degree convention; sketching and recovering equations; fitting/using real models and comparing conditions |
| 7 Discrete Functions | 7.1–7.7 | C1.1–C1.6, C2.1–C2.4 | Discrete domain; arithmetic/geometric terms and sums; explicit versus recursive rules; Fibonacci and second differences; Pascal patterns and binomial expansions; applications |
| 7 Financial Applications | F1–F6 (Nelson 8.1–8.6) | C3.1–C3.7 | Simple interest; compound FV/PV; ordinary simple annuity FV/PV; matching payment and compounding periods; technology to solve rate/time/payment and compare scenarios |
